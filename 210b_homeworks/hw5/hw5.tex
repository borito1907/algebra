\section{Problem 5}

\begin{lemma}
    Let $ R $ be a domain. If every module over $ R $ is free, $ R $ is a field.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
    Suppose not. Then, $ R $ has a non-zero non-unit ideal $ I $.
    Consider the $ R $ module $ R/I $. But free modules over domains have to be
    torsion-free and therefore $ R/I $ isn't free.
\end{proof}
